Adapting the methods shown in the text let $G(z)$ and $F(z)$ be the generating functions of the sequences $\langle2^n\rangle$ and $\langle3^n\rangle$, respectively. Hence

$$
\begin{gather}
    G(z)=1+2z+2^2z^2+\cdots\\
    2zG(z)=2z+2^2z^2+2^3z^3+\cdots\\
    G(z)-2zG(z)=1\\
    (1-2z)G(z)=1\\
    G(z)=\frac{1}{1-2z}.
\end{gather}
$$

Similarly,

$$
    F(z)=\frac{1}{1-3z}.
$$

Therefore the generating function of the sequence $\langle2^n+3^n\rangle$ is

$$
    \frac{1}{1-2z}+\frac{1}{1-3z}=\frac{1-2z+1-3z}{(1-2z)(1-3z)}=\frac{2-5z}{1-5z+6z^2}.
$$
